# 05 · 视频广告：制作台 ＋ 三组分镜

> 对应视频章节：4/7 会动的视频广告

## 第一步：广告制作台（Claude Design · Animation）

首页 → **Template** 选 **Animation** → Design system 选好。

```
帮我做一个广告制作台，要能产出Facebook、Meta、Instagram的各种广告格式，
包含会动的视频广告跟静态广告。

我等一下会上传背景用的图片跟视频。现在请你先：
1. 把动态效果的部分都做好
2. 加上可以现场调整的开关，让我能换尺寸、换图片、换视频
3. 告诉我你需要我生哪些图片跟视频，以及它们的尺寸
4. 先用SVG做出开场跟结尾的动画
```

问卷回答（视频里的选择）：尺寸只勾 **9:16**、时长 **15秒**、叙事选 **「问题→出动→解决」**、以视频为主、先做一版。

结果：15 秒切成五场（Opening 2.5s → Problem 3.5s → Dispatch 3.5s → Solution 3s → Ending 2.5s）。开场结尾是纯 SVG 品牌动画，中间三场留槽位。文案（如「凌晨两点，水管爆了？」）是它照设计系统里定义的**品牌语气**写的——设计系统管的不只是颜色字体，连品牌怎么说话都管。

## 第二步：有故事的分镜（另开普通 Claude 对话）

它给的默认素材提示词太普通（「一个漂亮的浴室」这种没人会停下来看）。广告要有效必须有转折：先痛苦、再解决。

```
帮我想几组图片生成提示词，这些图之后会拿去生成视频，用在水电维修公司的广告上。

我要的是有转折的：从水管爆裂、屋主很困扰的痛苦画面，
一路到最后问题解决、屋主很满意的画面。
可以有好几个分镜。给我比"漂亮的浴室"更有故事感的提示词。
```

它给六个分镜（爆发、困扰、救援、修复、解决、满意），并设计了**冷色调走到暖色调**的色彩转变。但 15 秒广告三个就够——挑**问题、救援、解决**，砍掉情绪重复的。

## 第三步：Lovart 生素材（三组完整提示词）

⚠️ 通用坑：

- **多张图人物要一致**：模型没有跨图记忆，**生完一张要当参考图传给下一张**。生第三张时传第二张（两个角色都在，一张锁两人）。
- **生视频提示词一定要写「不要对白」**（no dialogue / mouths closed）——不然模型会自己配英文台词，跟后期文案打架。
- 全部 **9:16 竖版**（投 Stories / Reels）。

### 第 1 组｜灾难爆发

**生图**（不传参考图——这张是基准图，多生几次挑最好的）：

```
CAMERA: low angle, positioned on the wet kitchen floor about two meters from the sink, looking slightly up at the man. Vertical 9:16 framing. The open sink cabinet with the burst pipe fills the LEFT half of the frame; the man occupies the RIGHT half, crouched low.

THE HOMEOWNER: a middle-aged white man in his 50s with short gray hair and gray stubble, wearing plaid flannel pajama pants and a plain gray t-shirt, barefoot. He is crouched down low on one knee, leaning forward, both hands gripping a white plastic bucket which he is thrusting under the spraying pipe. His face is turned up and toward the camera, mouth open, eyes wide — genuine panic and helplessness, not acting. His shirt and forearms are already soaked. His body is tense, off-balance, caught in a moment of urgency.

THE WATER: a copper pipe under the sink has burst at the joint. Water sprays out sideways in a forceful, chaotic jet, hitting the inside of the cabinet door and splashing outward. Individual droplets are frozen in the air, catching the light. A spreading pool of water covers the tile floor, reflecting the window light. Soaked towels lie crumpled uselessly on the floor around him.

SETTING: interior of a modest American Midwest kitchen. Dark wood cabinets, worn tile floor, a window on the left casting harsh, cold early-morning light across the wet floor. Everything reads as cheap, real, lived-in — not a showroom kitchen.

MOOD & LIGHT: cool blue color grade, high contrast, harsh directional light from the window, deep shadows under the cabinets. Cinematic, tense, chaotic. Shallow depth of field — the man and the water jet are sharp, the background falls off softly. Photorealistic, sharp detail on the water droplets.

No text, no logos, no branding anywhere.
```

**生视频**（上传刚生好的图当首帧）：

```
The water continues to spray forcefully and chaotically from the burst copper pipe, splashing against the cabinet and the bucket, spreading across the tile floor. The pool of water visibly grows.

The homeowner struggles to hold the bucket steady against the pressure of the jet. He flinches as water splashes his arms, and keeps his desperate, panicked expression fixed toward the camera. Mouth closed, no speaking. His movements are urgent and unsteady.

Camera: handheld, slight nervous shake, holding on the man and the spraying water. Tense, chaotic energy.

Cool blue color grade throughout. No dialogue, no voiceover, ambient sound only.
```

### 第 2 组｜救援抵达

**生图**（📎 上传「图 1」当参考图）：

```
Use the man from the reference image — the same middle-aged white man in his 50s with short gray hair and gray stubble, wearing the same plaid pajamas.

CAMERA: positioned inside the house, in the hallway, looking back toward the open front door. The front door is at the LEFT edge of the frame. The hallway extends toward the camera and deeper to the RIGHT, where the flooded kitchen is (visible as a wet, cool-blue lit area in the background right).

THE PLUMBER: a man in his 30s with short brown hair, wearing a plain navy blue work shirt and navy trousers with no logos, carrying a plain gray toolbox in his right hand. He is captured MID-STRIDE, walking INTO the house — his left foot has landed on the hallway floor inside, his right foot is still behind him on the outside doorstep, weight shifted forward. His torso, shoulders, hips, and face are all turned AWAY from the door and TOWARD the interior of the house, angled to the RIGHT of the frame, looking in the direction he is walking. His whole body reads as moving forward and to the right, deeper into the house. He is NOT facing the camera. He is NOT standing still.

THE HOMEOWNER: standing further inside the house, in the RIGHT half of the frame, ahead of the plumber along his path. He is turned to face the plumber, one arm extended, urgently pointing PAST the plumber's path, deeper to the RIGHT toward the flooded kitchen. His body language is urgent, relieved that help has arrived.

SPACE: a clear, unobstructed stretch of hallway floor in front of the plumber, leading to the right and into the depth of the house. Nothing blocks his path. The room must have visible depth so there is somewhere for him to walk to.

SETTING: same modest American Midwest house interior with dark wood trim. Warm entryway light on the plumber's back from the open door behind him, contrasting the cool blue flooded kitchen light deeper inside. Low camera angle looking slightly up at the plumber, heroic framing. Strong sense of urgent forward motion and arrival. Photorealistic, cinematic, vertical 9:16 composition.

No text, no logos, no branding anywhere.
```

**生视频**（上传刚生好的图当首帧）：

> 🔴 **翻车记录**：图里屋主面朝师傅、手指着他，师傅要往前走、屋主又挡在路上 → 模型只能让屋主**倒退让路**，看起来像被威胁逼退。
> ✅ **解法：让屋主转身带路**。关键三招：①动作拆两拍（放下手→转身→迈步）②用 `showing his back` 这种**正面描述**，比「不要倒退」有效 ③明确两人**同向**走。

```
The homeowner drops his pointing arm and TURNS AROUND to face the kitchen, then walks forward into the kitchen, leading the way. He is walking FORWARD, moving away from the camera, showing his back — he is NOT walking backwards, he is NOT retreating, he never steps backward at any point.

The plumber follows behind him, walking forward into the house with a steady, confident stride, carrying his toolbox. Both men walk in the SAME direction, away from the camera, deeper into the house toward the flooded kitchen. Neither man turns back toward the camera.

Both mouths closed, no speaking.

Camera: slowly pushes forward, following both men from behind as they move deeper into the hallway toward the kitchen. Smooth, steady tracking motion.

The warm entryway light gives way to the cool blue light of the flooded kitchen ahead. No dialogue, no voiceover, ambient sound only.
```

> 💡 如果还是倒退：重生图 2，让屋主**从图上就已经半转身**——视频只需「把转身完成」，比从正面转 180° 容易得多。生图 prompt 的 THE HOMEOWNER 段改成：
>
> ```
> THE HOMEOWNER: standing in the kitchen doorway, his body already TURNED toward the kitchen, showing his back and shoulder to the camera, glancing back over his shoulder at the plumber while pointing INTO the kitchen with his near arm. He is mid-turn, about to lead the way in.
> ```

### 第 3 组｜满意收尾

**生图**（📎 上传「图 2」当参考图——两个角色都在，一张锁两人）：

```
Use both people from the reference image — the same middle-aged white man in his 50s with short gray hair and gray stubble, and the same plumber in his 30s with short brown hair wearing the same plain navy blue work shirt and navy trousers with no logos.

CAMERA: eye-level, positioned in the driveway a few meters back, looking toward the front door of the house. Vertical 9:16 framing. The two men stand together in the center of the frame; the front door of the house is behind them on the RIGHT; the parked van is visible behind them on the LEFT.

THE TWO MEN: they face each other in profile, standing close, mid-handshake — hands clasped firmly between them, both arms extended. The homeowner has changed out of his pajamas into a casual knit sweater and jeans; he is relaxed now, shoulders down, smiling warmly, looking directly at the plumber's face. The plumber stands squarely, confident and easy, smiling back, his free hand relaxed at his side, the toolbox on the ground beside his feet. Both men's mouths are closed — they are smiling, not talking. Their body language reads as trust, gratitude, and a job well done.

THE VAN: a plain white work van parked in the driveway behind them, angled slightly away. The van's side panel is completely blank — pure white, no text, no logo, no graphics, no lettering of any kind.

SETTING: exterior of a modest American Midwest house with white clapboard siding, a simple covered porch, a small tidy front lawn. Everything is dry and calm now — no trace of the flood.

MOOD & LIGHT: warm golden-hour sunlight, low and soft, coming from the side and rim-lighting both men. Long gentle shadows across the driveway. Warm, calm, reassuring. The color grade is the opposite of the earlier cold blue — this is warm amber and gold. Wide, open, inviting composition. Photorealistic, cinematic.

No text, no logos, no branding anywhere. The van must be completely blank.
```

**生视频**（上传刚生好的图当首帧）：

```
The two men complete their handshake — hands release slowly and naturally. Both smile and nod once to each other, mouths closed, no speaking, no talking. The homeowner gives a small appreciative nod. Their movements are calm, slow, and relaxed. They remain standing in place.

Camera: slowly and smoothly pulls back, gradually revealing more of the house, the driveway, the toolbox on the ground, and the white van. Steady, cinematic motion — no shake.

Warm golden-hour backlight throughout, calm and satisfied mood. No dialogue, no voiceover, ambient sound only.
```

## 第四步：把素材填进槽位

广告制作台的 Tweaks 面板已经把槽位命名好了：`uploads/problem.mp4`、`uploads/dispatch.mp4`、`uploads/solution.jpg`。

1. 在 Finder 把三支视频**改名成它要的名字**：爆管 → `problem.mp4`、师傅进门 → `dispatch.mp4`、握手 → `solution.mp4`
2. 三支全部拖进 Claude Design 上传
3. ⚠️ 「解决场景」那栏默认写的是 `.jpg`（它以为你放图），有视频就把路径改成 `uploads/solution.mp4`——它会自动识别
4. 点 Reload，播放完整 15 秒广告
